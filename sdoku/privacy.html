광고 데이터의 처리 범위는 지역, 동의 여부, 기기 및 광고 설정에 따라 달라집니다. 광고 SDK 전송은 TLS로 암호화됩니다. Google의 보관·처리·삭제 정책은 https://policies.google.com/privacy 및 https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads 에서 확인할 수 있습니다. 광고 데이터는 Google 및 관련 서비스의 해외 인프라에서 처리될 수 있으며, 국가별 추가 고지나 동의가 필요한 경우 실제 서비스 구성에 맞게 제공합니다.

4. 선택과 삭제

필요한 지역에서는 광고 동의 화면을 제공하고, 다시 선택할 수 있는 경우 앱 설정에 ‘광고 개인정보 선택’을 표시합니다. 광고 관련 선택을 거부하거나 광고를 불러오지 못해도 스도쿠를 플레이할 수 있습니다. 기기 설정에서 광고 ID 관련 선택을 변경할 수 있습니다. Android 설정에서 SDOKU의 앱 데이터를 삭제하거나 앱을 삭제하면 기기에 저장된 진행과 기록을 삭제할 수 있습니다. 계정이 없으므로 별도의 계정 삭제 절차는 없습니다.

This policy applies to SDOKU Sudoku - One-Move Coach, also listed as SDOKU 스도쿠 - 한 수 코치, for Android. The operator is 로고스 스튜디오 (Logos Studio). Contact: whwodnr153@gmail.com. Effective date: October 17, 2026.

2. Data stored on your device

No account is required. Puzzle progress, entries, notes, undo history, mistakes, hints, time, results, daily challenges and settings are stored on your device. Minimal local events record app opens, game starts, completions, game overs and coach use with dates and times. Puzzle entries and these events are not automatically uploaded to the operator. The app has no cloud sync or remote analytics service.

Progress and settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Local events are trimmed against a maximum 400-day retention window when the app starts. Android app backup is disabled; uninstalling removes local progress without a cloud recovery option.

3. Advertising services

Google Mobile Ads SDK provides a bottom banner, and Google User Messaging Platform handles advertising privacy choices. For advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and diagnostics, Google may collect and share IP addresses (which may estimate approximate location), app/ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID. The app does not request precise-location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Processing varies with region, consent, device and advertising settings. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's processing, retention and deletion practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Google and related services may process data on infrastructure outside your country. Any additional regional notices or consent must reflect the actual service configuration.

4. Choices and deletion

Where required, the app presents advertising consent and exposes Advertising privacy choices in Settings when available. Declining an advertising choice or failing to load an ad does not block Sudoku. Device settings provide advertising-ID controls. Clearing SDOKU app data or uninstalling deletes locally stored progress and records. There is no account to delete.

5. Sharing and support email

Results or local usage summaries are shared only when you use the share action and choose a destination. That destination's privacy policy applies. Support emails provide your address and message so we can respond. Do not include unnecessary personal information or authentication secrets.
